# V6.4 — Graphical Mechanism Engine

V6.4 retains V6.3 general ROS recognition and adds a browser-safe graphical mechanism layer.

## New
- `intermediate_structure_generator.py`: conservative mechanism-stage model; never invents a molecular graph when no validated SMILES exists.
- `mechanism_path_renderer.py`: PNG mechanism-pathway renderer with chemical structures, reaction arrows, step labels, and descriptions.
- `reaction_center_visualizer.py`: highlights mapped reaction-center atoms on reactant and product structures.
- `curved_arrow_renderer.py`: reusable PIL curved-arrow primitive for verified electron-flow annotations.
- Main Streamlit UI now shows a graphical mechanism pathway before the written mechanism.
- Existing V6.3 independent-upload/session reset and no-hard-coded-fallback behavior is retained.

## Important limitation
Exact intermediate structures and curved electron-flow arrows require validated molecular graphs and atom anchors. V6.4 does not fabricate structures from text-only mechanism labels. When an intermediate graph is not established, the graphical pathway marks it as **Structure pending verification**.
